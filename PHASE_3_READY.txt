  Required: 120 units
  
  Available Batches:
    Batch A: 100 units, expires 2026-08-01
    Batch B: 50 units, expires 2026-07-15 (earliest)
    Batch C: 75 units, expires 2026-09-01

FEFO Allocation Result:
  1. Batch B: 50 units (earliest expiry first)
  2. Batch A: 70 units (second earliest)
  Total: 120 units allocated

Benefits:
  ✓ Prevents waste from expired products
  ✓ Healthcare compliance (FIFO/FEFO)
  ✓ Automatic optimal selection
